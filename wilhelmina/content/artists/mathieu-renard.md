Mathieu Renard is a french artist and publisher living in Rennes. In 2003, he founded Lendroit éditions, a publishing house, bookstore and gallery entirely dedicated to artists’ publishing. His artistic work focuses on drawing, cutting and editing, in a practice that plays on multiple references.
